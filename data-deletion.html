Delete your data

Khalas has no account to delete. Your routine data stays on your device.

Delete routines, steps, and logs

Uninstall Khalas from your Android device. Android deletes the app’s local SQLite database, including your routines, steps, completion logs, and settings.

This is permanent. Export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

What HabibiApps stores

HabibiApps runs no Khalas server or backend and does not receive your routine data. We therefore have no remote copy of your routines, steps, or logs to find or delete.

Subscription and purchase records

RevenueCat and Google Play may retain subscription and transaction records separately from your local routine data. To request deletion or exercise a privacy right concerning those records, email khalas@habibiapps.com with the subject “Khalas purchase data deletion.” We will explain the information needed to locate the relevant anonymous purchase record and direct the request to the appropriate provider.

You may also use the privacy-request options provided directly by RevenueCat and Google. Some transaction records may need to be retained where required for tax, fraud-prevention, accounting, or other legal obligations.

Deleting the app does not cancel Premium

Uninstalling Khalas deletes local app data, but it does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
