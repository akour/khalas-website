What it is

Small chains.
One step at a time.

A routine is a short chain of things you want to do: wake up, drink water, take your meds, brush your teeth.

Tap each step as you do it. Today shows the chains for your day. Routines lets you edit them. Progress keeps your history in a heatmap. Settings keeps the rest out of your way.

Less pressure. More clarity.

FAQ

The useful answers.

Does Khalas sync between devices?

No. Your routine data stays locally on your device and is not uploaded.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing. There is no punishment, reset, or message waiting for you.

Is my routine data private?

Yes. It stays in an on-device database. Khalas has no accounts, analytics, ad SDKs, tracking, or backend.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your routines, steps, and logs to a file and choose where to share it.
