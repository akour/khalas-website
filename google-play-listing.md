## Full description

Khalas is a quiet routine app built for ADHD.

A routine is a small chain of steps: wake up, drink water, take your meds, brush your teeth. Khalas shows you the chain and lets you tap each step as you do it.

No bottomless lists. No punishment. Just the next step.

YOUR WHOLE DAY, ON ONE SCREEN

Today shows all of the routine chains for your day. Nothing important is buried. Nothing keeps scrolling forever.

ONE DECISION AT A TIME

The next step you could do is the only one competing for your attention. Complete it, then move to the next.

NO SHAME MECHANICS

An unfinished day does not become a failure before the day is over. If you miss a day, nothing punishes you.

STREAKS WITHOUT THE PRESSURE

You can see your streaks and history. Breaking a streak does not reset your progress or take anything away.

QUIET REMINDERS

Choose one reminder time each day. The reminder is scheduled locally on your phone. Ignore it and nothing else happens.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS

The Progress screen gives you a clear history heatmap without turning your routines into a competition.

MAKE ROUTINES YOURS

Create a chain, add its steps, and edit it when life changes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No account or sign-up
• Routine data stays locally on your device
• No analytics or tracking
• No ads
• No cloud backend
• Export your routines, steps, and logs whenever you choose

FOUR SIMPLE SCREENS

• Today — see and complete today’s chains
• Routines — create and edit routines
• Progress — review your history heatmap
• Settings — manage the app and reminders

FREE AND PREMIUM

The free tier includes one routine and seven days of history.

Premium unlocks unlimited routines and full history through a Google Play subscription.

Khalas means “enough.”

You do not need a perfect system. You need a clear next step.
