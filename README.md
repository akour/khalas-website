# Khalas website

Static launch website and Google Play compliance pages for Khalas by HabibiApps.

Designed to be served at `https://habibiapps.com/khalas/`.

## Pages

- `index.html` — landing page
- `privacy.html` — privacy policy
- `terms.html` — terms of service
- `data-deletion.html` — data deletion instructions
- `support.html` — support and FAQ
- `google-play-listing.md` — Play Store copy
- `data-safety.md` — Play Console Data Safety mapping

## Before launch

Replace `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]` in `privacy.html` and `terms.html`.
